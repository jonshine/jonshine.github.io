Market Failures Caused by Externalities and Asymmetric Information

Markets sometimes fail to allocate resources efficiently. This chapter examines two major sources of market failure: externalities, where costs or benefits spill over onto third parties, and asymmetric information, where one party to a transaction knows more than the other.
